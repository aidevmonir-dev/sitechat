Add it to any website with one line

<script src="{{ base_url }}/widget.js" data-site="your-site" async></script>
  • Answers only from your content. You write short help articles; the assistant does not make up prices or policies.
  • Hands over when it should. If a visitor asks for a person, or the articles do not cover the question, it collects a name and email and notifies you.
  • Works on any site. WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or hand-written HTML. The widget is isolated from your page's styles.
  • Private by design. No cookies and no tracking. Conversations are deleted after {{ retention_days }} days.
